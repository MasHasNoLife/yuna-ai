Here is a detailed guide on CSing (Creep Score) in League of Legends for you to explain to the audience:

1. What is CS and Why It Matters
- CS stands for Creep Score, which is the number of minions and monsters you last-hit.
- It is the most reliable and consistent source of gold and experience in the game. 
- Roughly 15 CS is equal to the gold of one champion kill (around 300g). People over-focus on getting kills, but a player with perfect CS will completely out-scale someone who is just coin-flipping fights.

2. The Mechanics of Last Hitting
- It's all about patience. You want to wait until the minion's health bar is low enough for your champion's auto-attack to secure the kill.
- You have to know your champion's attack animation speed and projectile travel time. A mage with a slow auto-attack needs to attack earlier than an ADC with a crisp, fast animation.
- Keep an eye on which enemy minion your own minions are focusing. That minion will drop in health the fastest.

3. CSing Under Tower
- This is a classic struggle but there are rules to it!
- Melee minions: Let the tower hit them twice, then you auto-attack once to secure it.
- Caster (Ranged) minions: These are squishier. The rule is you auto-attack them once, let the tower hit them once, then auto-attack again to finish them off. (If you have enough damage or items, you can just wait for the tower to hit them once and then finish them).

4. Balancing Trading and CSing
- Don't just tunnel vision on the minions. Watch the enemy laner.
- The best time to hit the enemy is when they step up to last-hit a minion, especially a valuable Cannon Minion. They are locked into their attack animation, meaning they can't hit you back immediately. 
- Conversely, be careful when you go for your own last hits, as good players will try to punish you for it.

5. Wave Management Basics
- Shoving: Killing minions as fast as possible to crash the wave into the enemy tower. Do this when you want to recall and buy items, so the enemy loses gold to the tower.
- Freezing: Only last-hitting at the very last second and keeping the wave close to your side of the lane. This forces the enemy to overextend into dangerous territory if they want to get gold, opening them up to ganks from your jungler.
